1%

A small share. A visible habit.

The 1% Influence Challenge invites creators to consider donating about 1% of creator income to a cause they choose, then encourage other creators to participate.

Creators donate directly to eligible charities during Phase 1. Influencers Involved does not take custody of participant donations.

What we are building

A trusted, practical pathway for creator-led giving.

The first phase is intentionally simple: no complicated accounts, no public disclosure of income, and no donation processing by Influencers Involved.

Mission

Make charitable giving a visible part of creator culture.

Digital creators shape language, trends, purchasing decisions, and social norms. Influencers Involved exists to help creators use that cultural influence to normalize charitable participation.

We are building a model that is easy to understand and respectful of privacy. A creator may choose a cause, donate directly, provide private proof for review, receive recognition for verified participation, and invite other creators to join.

Generosity becomes more powerful when people can see it, repeat it, and invite others into it.

Who we serve

  • Social-media creators and independent digital publishers
  • Eligible charities selected by participating creators
  • Audiences interested in credible social-impact participation
  • Brands, agencies, and funders supporting creator-led philanthropy

Initial approach

The organization is designed for nationwide participation, with an initial Chicago-area proof point to test outreach, verification, and measurement.

Program in development

The 1% Influence Challenge

Make a pledge

A creator chooses a voluntary charitable commitment that fits their circumstances.

Choose an eligible charity

The creator selects an organization aligned with their values.

Donate directly

The creator gives directly to the selected charity. Influencers Involved does not receive the participant donation.

Submit appropriate proof privately

The creator may provide documentation for confidential review without publicly disclosing income or donation amount.

Receive recognition and invite others

After review, II may recognize verified participation and provide materials for inviting other creators.

Example pledge language

“I’m joining the 1% Influence Challenge by making charitable giving part of my creator work—and inviting other creators to do the same.”

Final pledge language, proof standards, and recognition rules will be published before enrollment opens.

Privacy commitment

A creator should be able to participate without publicly disclosing personal income or an exact donation amount.

About

A founder-led nonprofit building a credible creator-giving movement.

Influencers Involved, Inc. NFP is an Illinois nonprofit corporation recognized by the IRS as a Section 501(c)(3) public charity.

Influencers Involved was established to connect the cultural reach of digital creators with practical charitable participation. The organization is currently in its pre-launch development phase and is preparing the first 1% Influence Challenge pilot.

Daily development is founder-led. The volunteer Board of Directors provides fiduciary, governance, financial, and compensation oversight.

Phase 1 financial boundary

Creators will donate directly to eligible charities. Influencers Involved will not take custody of, retain a percentage of, or redistribute those participant donations during Phase 1 unless a future legal and compliance review approves a different model.

As operations begin, II intends to publish appropriate public records and updates, including annual filings and program results. Sensitive personal, banking, participant, and verification information will not be published.
