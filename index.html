The 1% Influence Challenge

Make 1% of your influence count.

Influencers Involved brings creators together to make giving part of creator culture. Pledge 1% of your creator income to a charity you care about, give directly to that charity, and inspire others to join.

How it works

One commitment. Your choice of cause.

01

Pledge + join

Choose a charity and join the Pioneer Registry. Your pledge is voluntary and does not require payment to join.

02

Give directly

After joining, make your gift directly to your selected charity, on your own schedule. II does not collect your 1%.

03

Share & invite

Tell your audience why you pledged. Invite other creators to turn their influence into good.

Pioneer recognition

Be part of the example.

Be among the creators who make generosity visible. Pioneers help build a culture in which charitable giving becomes a repeatable, public example—not a one-time campaign.

Participation starts with a pledge; how you share is up to you.

Become a Pioneer

Recognition on your terms

Receive an optional shareable pledge certificate. Public listing is always your choice, and appears only after email verification.

Our role

We bring people together.

We organize the 1% Influence Challenge, celebrate voluntary pledges, and help creators inspire one another. In future II-organized charitable campaigns, creators may also rally their audiences and corporate supporters to give directly to a charity the creator selects.
