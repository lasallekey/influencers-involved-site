Mission

Make charitable giving a visible part of creator culture.

Creators shape habits, language, purchasing decisions, and social norms. Influencers Involved exists to help creators normalize charitable participation and inspire others through visible, repeatable action.

The organization is preparing a nationwide initiative with an initial Chicago-area proof point focused on creator participation, privacy-conscious pledge verification, and measurable charitable reach.

Creator choice

Participants choose a cause or eligible charity aligned with their own values.

Privacy-conscious participation

Creators need not publicly disclose income or exact donation amounts.

Social multiplication

Every pledge becomes an invitation for another creator to participate.

Transparency

Clear about what exists now—and what comes next.

Influencers Involved is an Illinois nonprofit corporation recognized by the IRS as a Section 501(c)(3) public charity. The first creator-philanthropy pilot is currently in development.

Phase 1

  • Creators make a voluntary pledge.
  • Participants donate directly to eligible charities.
  • Influencers Involved does not take custody of participant donations.
  • The public certificate does not disclose income or donation amount.
  • No public claim of completed impact is made without evidence.

Future platform development

Influencers Involved may later offer an optional integrated giving experience that combines charitable giving with support for verification, technology, outreach, and impact reporting.

Any future allocation or payment structure will be introduced only after legal, accounting, governance, and compliance review and will be clearly disclosed before participation.

About the organization

Built for credible creator-led philanthropy.

Influencers Involved is developing the 1% Influence Challenge, creator outreach systems, a privacy-conscious proof-review process, charity-vetting standards, and an outcomes framework for launch.

Operations are founder-led and governed by a volunteer Board of Directors with fiduciary, financial, governance, and compensation oversight.
