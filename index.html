How the movement grows

The Pioneer Stage creates the foundation. Then we move forward together.

II provides the structure, tools, recognition, and stewardship. Pioneers provide the action that makes the movement real.

Stage One · Now

The Pioneer Stage

  • Pioneers make a voluntary public pledge.
  • They donate directly to eligible charities they choose.
  • They create visible proof that creator-led giving can become a shared norm.
  • They invite other creators and build the first circle of participation.
  • Their collective action determines the strength of the movement.
Stage Two · What pioneers unlock

The Connected Giving Model

Once enough pioneers have shown that creators are ready to make this happen together, the challenge will move into its next model.

That next model will provide a more connected experience designed to make participation easier to complete, recognize, measure, and grow at scale.

The transition is not something pioneers need to solve. Their role is to lead through action. II's role is to build the next structure around the momentum they create.

Mission

Make charitable giving a visible part of creator culture.

Creators shape habits, language, purchasing decisions, and social norms. Influencers Involved exists to help creators normalize charitable participation and inspire others through visible, repeatable action.

This is creator-led philanthropy. II organizes the challenge and builds the infrastructure, but pioneers are the people who produce the charitable action and social momentum.

Creator choice

Pioneers choose a cause or eligible charity aligned with their own values.

Privacy-conscious participation

Creators need not publicly disclose income or exact donation amounts.

Social multiplication

Every Pioneer Pledge becomes an invitation for another creator to participate.

About the organization

Infrastructure for a movement led by creators.

Influencers Involved is developing the 1% Influence Challenge, creator outreach systems, privacy-conscious participation and proof-review processes, charity-vetting standards, and an outcomes framework.

Operations are founder-led and governed by a volunteer Board of Directors with fiduciary, financial, governance, and compensation oversight.
