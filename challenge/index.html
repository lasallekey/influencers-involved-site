The Pioneer Stage

Three steps to become a Pioneer

01

Pledge

Choose a charity and pledge 1% of your creator income. You decide the amount calculation and when to give; II does not ask for your income or payment details.

02

Join

Enter your details and social accounts, choose your public display preference, and submit.

03

Get recognized

After confirmed registration, receive your Pioneer ID and an optional pledge certificate.

After joining

Two independent actions

Give directly

Donate to your chosen charity through its own giving channel. II does not process or redistribute the 1% pledge. Proof is not required for Pioneer status.

Share & invite

Create a post in your own voice, share your pledge or certificate if you wish, and encourage fellow creators to participate.

Privacy and trust

Recognition is your choice.

Pioneer recognition is based on a pledge, not donation verification. You choose whether to appear publicly; anonymous Pioneers are counted but never identified. We may ask you to verify your email to protect the registry; verification does not delay your pledge certificate.

No proof required

We do not collect the 1% gift, request income details, or ask for donation receipts.

Pioneer Registry

Creators who choose to lead by example

Only verified creators who choose public recognition can appear here. A creator with several social accounts is still one Pioneer. Private contact details and collaboration interests are never published.

Public Pioneer listings will appear after the new secure registration service is launched and eligible participants choose to be listed.
