Our mission

Encourage creators to use their influence to inspire meaningful charitable giving, beginning with a voluntary 1% pledge to the charity each creator chooses.

Our first program

The 1% Influence Challenge helps creators make a pledge, join the Pioneer Registry, donate directly to their chosen cause, and invite others.

Explore the Challenge

Small, visible commitments can spark generosity.

Creators connect with people every day. Small visible commitments can spark more generous behavior across communities.

We built Influencers Involved to give creators a straightforward way to make that commitment public without asking us to hold their pledged donations. As participation grows, we hope to organize future campaigns that help raise funds for causes chosen by participating creators.
