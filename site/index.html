Free to join. No donation, income figure or receipt required.

It starts with a pledge. Then it spreads.

Three steps, at your own pace.

  1. Pledge and join

    Pick a charity you care about and join the Pioneer Registry. It's voluntary, and joining costs nothing.

  2. Give directly

    Make your gift straight to your charity, whenever you choose. We never touch your 1%.

  3. Share and invite

    Tell your community why you joined, and invite other creators to come with you, if you'd like.
