GivingTuesday 2026 · December 1

Editorial

GivingTuesday brings many kinds of generosity into focus. If you're wondering how to take part, how to choose a cause, or how to share a campaign responsibly, start here. We explain the essentials, then look closely at four animal-related organizations with published plans for December 1, 2026.

These are independent articles by Influencers Involved, Inc. NFP. We are not the organizer of GivingTuesday, and none of the featured charities is presented as our partner or endorser. When you choose to donate to a featured charity, use that charity's official giving page.
