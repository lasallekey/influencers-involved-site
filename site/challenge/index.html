Become a Pioneer

Program overview

The 1% Influence Challenge

A simple pledge. A charity you choose. A ripple effect you create. Join other creators in making charitable giving visible by pledging 1% of your creator income to a cause that matters to you.

Become a Pioneer See Pioneer recognition No donation is required to register.

The Pioneer stage, in order

Pledge, join, get recognized.

  1. 1

    Pledge

    Choose a charity and pledge 1% of your creator income. You decide the amount calculation and when to give; II does not ask for your income or payment details.

  2. 2

    Join

    Enter your details and social accounts, select a public display preference, and submit.

  3. 3

    Get recognized

    After successful registration, receive your Pioneer ID and an optional pledge certificate.

After you join

Two independent actions.

Give directly

Donate to your chosen charity through its own giving channel. II does not process or redistribute the 1% pledge. Proof is not required for Pioneer status.

Share & invite

Create a post in your own voice, share your pledge or certificate if you wish, and encourage fellow creators to participate.

Privacy and trust

A pledge, not a payment.

Pioneer recognition is based on a pledge, not donation verification. You choose whether to appear publicly; anonymous Pioneers are counted but never identified. We may ask you to verify your email to protect the registry; verification does not delay your pledge certificate. Read the privacy notice or how giving works.
