Privacy notice

Privacy & Pioneer participation

This notice describes the information Influencers Involved collects through Pioneer registration, how public recognition works, and how you can contact us about your information.

Information we collect

Only what the program needs.

To administer Pioneer registration, II may collect creator name, private email, country, selected charity and pledge date, social profiles, public-display choices, optional communication and collaboration interests, and verification status. II does not request income amounts or proof of giving.

What may be public

Public recognition is optional.

You control public recognition. Only a verified, publicly opted-in Pioneer and explicitly approved profiles may appear in public listings. Anonymous records contribute only to privacy-safe aggregate totals. Email addresses and collaboration responses are never displayed.

Private communication

Separate choices for future contact.

We may send essential registration and verification messages to administer your pledge. Optional news emails and collaboration invitations each require their own unchecked opt-in; you may unsubscribe or withdraw either permission. Email verification does not constitute consent to marketing.

Corrections and withdrawal

Questions about your data?

To request correction, removal, or consent withdrawal, contact grants@influencersinvolved.org.

Pre-launch disclosure review: The precise database provider, email sender, retention period, technical safeguards and any tracking or cookies will be stated here once the registration service is deployed and verified. This draft does not promise end-to-end encryption.
