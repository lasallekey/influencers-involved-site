Transparency

How the Challenge works — and how giving works

We want creators, donors, and partners to understand exactly what Influencers Involved does, what it does not do, and how we respect participants’ choices.

Two distinct kinds of giving

The creator’s 1% pledge

The creator selects a charity and donates directly to it. During the Pioneer Stage, Influencers Involved does not collect, process, or redistribute the creator’s pledged 1%.

Donations to Influencers Involved

Gifts through the Donate page go to Influencers Involved, Inc. NFP and support its nonprofit work, outreach, technology, and program development.

Honest participation standards

Recognition is not a donation receipt.

The Pioneer Stage is voluntary and trust-based. Creators may join before making their direct donation, and no proof is required. A Pioneer Certificate documents the pledge only; it is not a charitable contribution receipt or evidence that any payment occurred.

Public recognition and counts

Your participation, your choice.

Public profiles require both creator permission and a verified email. Anonymous Pioneers count in aggregate but are not identified. II does not publish private email, collaboration interests, or unapproved social profiles. Registration records are not proof of donations.
