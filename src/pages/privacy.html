Your information, your choices.

Here’s what we collect when you join the Pioneer Registry, what can be public, and how to reach us about your information.

  • We never publish your email. Only a verified, publicly opted-in Pioneer and the profiles they approve can appear in public listings.
  • You decide what’s public. Anonymous Pioneers are counted in totals but never identified.
  • Optional emails are always your choice. News and collaboration invitations are separate, unchecked opt-ins you can withdraw at any time.

Who can join

Phase 1 of the Pioneer Registry is open to adults aged 18 and older, and the registration form asks you to confirm that. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a young person has registered, write to us and we will remove the record.

Information we collect

To administer Pioneer registration, II collects your creator name, private email, country, selected charity (or a note that you haven’t chosen one yet) and pledge date, the social accounts you list (a profile link is optional), your public-display choice and per-account approvals, optional communication and collaboration choices, and email-verification status. We also record the date and version of the choices you agreed to. II does not request income amounts, payment details or proof of giving.

To limit abuse, the registration service briefly keeps a one-way, keyed (hashed) form of your network address, with a request count, to rate-limit requests. It is not your address, is not used to identify you, and is deleted automatically.

This site sets no advertising cookies. It sets analytics cookies only if you accept them (see Analytics and cookies), and it stores one small preference entry in your browser to remember your cookie choice.

What may be public

You control public recognition. Only a verified, publicly opted-in Pioneer, and only the social profiles that Pioneer explicitly approved, may appear in public listings. Anonymous records contribute only to privacy-safe aggregate totals; public totals appear only once enough Pioneers have joined that no individual stands out. Your email address, country, collaboration responses and verification details are never displayed.

A downloaded certificate shows your creator name only if you chose public recognition; otherwise it reads “Anonymous Pioneer”.

Private information and communications

We send the essential registration and email-verification messages needed to administer your pledge. Verification only confirms you control the inbox; it is not consent to marketing. Optional news emails and collaboration invitations each require their own unchecked opt-in, may be withdrawn at any time, and every optional message includes an unsubscribe link. Answering “Maybe” or “Yes” to collaboration does not by itself allow us to email you.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Analytics and cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4, a service of Google LLC, to count visits and see which pages and steps are useful so we can improve the site. It is off until you choose “Accept analytics” in the banner shown on your first visit. “Reject analytics” is just as available, and rejecting never limits anything you can do on this site.

What is collected, only if you accept. Two first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_K34TWL28XS, which last up to two years) hold a random identifier so repeat visits can be recognized. Google Analytics then receives the address of the pages you view (without any query string or # fragment), the page title, the page you came from, a few simple events (starting the Pioneer form, saving a pledge, confirming your email, downloading a certificate, clicking a donation link), and technical details such as browser, device type, language and an approximate location that Google derives from your connection. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses.

What we never send. We do not send your name, email address, social handles, charity, Pioneer ID, certificate, verification link or any other registration information to Google Analytics, and we never send the part of an address after a “?” or “#”, so verification and unsubscribe links are not shared. We switch off Google’s advertising features: no advertising cookies, no Google signals and no ad personalization.

How long. The user-level and event-level data in our Google Analytics property (which includes individual visits and the events listed above) is deleted automatically after 2 months. Standard aggregated reports, which show totals and trends and are not tied to a person, may remain available for longer. Your cookie choice is remembered in your browser’s local storage on this device only (it is never sent to us or to Google) until you clear it.

Your choices. Use Cookie preferences at the bottom of every page to change or withdraw your choice at any time. Choosing “Reject analytics” deletes the Google Analytics cookies and stops sending data. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal we treat it as “Reject”, even if you accepted earlier: analytics stays off, we remove any Google Analytics cookies already set, and “Accept analytics” is unavailable until you turn the signal off. You can also block cookies in your browser settings or use Google’s opt-out add-on. Data already collected is not tied to your name and is deleted when it expires. Google acts as our service provider for this measurement and may process data in the United States and other countries; see Google’s privacy policy.

Service providers

We use a few providers to run the registry. Each handles information only for the purpose described here and under its own terms and privacy policy.

  • Cloudflare hosts this website, runs the registration service and stores registration records in its D1 database, which is encrypted at rest by the provider and accessed over TLS. This is provider-managed encryption, not end-to-end encryption. Only the registration service and authorized II administrators can read private records. As our hosting and network provider, Cloudflare also processes visitors’ network addresses to deliver and protect the site and may keep short-lived operational logs.
  • Resend delivers our verification and optional emails. It receives your email address, your name and the message content, and keeps delivery records under its own policy.
  • Google (Google Analytics 4) measures website visits only if you accept analytics; see Analytics and cookies. It never receives registration information.
  • Zeffy processes donations on its own site under its own privacy policy. II never sees or stores your card details. Donating is separate from Pioneer registration and is not linked to it.
  • Google Workspace handles the email you send to {{email}}.

The social links in our footer lead to other companies’ sites, which have their own privacy practices.

How long we keep information

  • Pioneer registration records are kept while you take part in the registry, subject to applicable law, until you ask us to remove them.
  • Email-verification links work once and expire after 24 hours. The stored (hashed) link record is deleted 7 days after it is used or expires; your record keeps only whether and when you verified.
  • Abuse-prevention counters (the hashed network address and request count) are deleted automatically, at most about a day after they are created.
  • Analytics data (only if you accept): user-level and event-level data is deleted automatically after 2 months in Google Analytics; aggregated reports that are not tied to a person may remain available longer.
  • Deletion requests. Verified requests to delete your registration are normally completed within 30 days. We may keep what the law requires or what is needed to resolve a dispute. Information we delete can remain in our database provider’s automatic recovery history for a limited period (currently up to 30 days) before it expires.

Your choices and international visitors

Wherever you live, you may ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, withdraw you from the registry, delete it, or withdraw an optional consent. Write to {{email}} from the email address you registered with (or tell us your Pioneer ID); we confirm it is really you before we act. You can unsubscribe from optional emails at any time with the link in each one. Depending on where you live, the law may give you additional rights, and you may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Influencers Involved is a nonprofit organization in the United States. Information you submit is sent to, stored and handled in the United States and in other countries where our providers operate, which may have different data-protection rules than your country. We use only the providers listed above.
