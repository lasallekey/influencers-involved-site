Privacy notice

Privacy & Pioneer participation

This notice describes the information Influencers Involved collects through Pioneer registration, how public recognition works, and how you can contact us about your information.

Information we collect

To administer Pioneer registration, II collects your creator name, private email, country, selected charity and pledge date, the social profiles you list, your public-display choice and per-account approvals, optional communication and collaboration choices, and email-verification status. We also record the date and version of the choices you agreed to. II does not request income amounts or proof of giving.

To limit abuse, the registration service briefly keeps a one-way (hashed) form of your network address to rate-limit requests. It is not used to identify you and is deleted automatically after a short period.

What may be public

You control public recognition. Only a verified, publicly opted-in Pioneer, and only the social profiles that Pioneer explicitly approved, may appear in public listings. Anonymous records contribute only to privacy-safe aggregate totals; breakdowns small enough to point to an individual are suppressed. Your email address, country, collaboration responses and verification details are never displayed.

A downloaded certificate shows your creator name only if you chose public recognition; otherwise it reads “Anonymous Pioneer”.

Private information and communications

We send the essential registration and email-verification messages needed to administer your pledge. Verification only confirms you control the inbox; it is not consent to marketing. Optional news emails and collaboration invitations each require their own unchecked opt-in, may be withdrawn at any time, and every optional message includes an unsubscribe link. Answering “Maybe” or “Yes” to collaboration does not by itself allow us to email you.

Corrections, withdrawal and data handling

Request correction, removal, or consent withdrawal through {{email}}. Registration records are held in a Cloudflare D1 database that is encrypted at rest by the provider and accessed over TLS; only the registration service and authorized II administrators can read private records. This is provider-managed encryption, not end-to-end encryption.

Verification emails are sent through a transactional email provider that II configures for its own domain. This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Donations are handled entirely by Zeffy on its own site under its own privacy policy.

Retention period and the final named email provider will be confirmed here before public launch.
