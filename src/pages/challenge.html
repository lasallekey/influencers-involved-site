The 1% Influence Challenge.

A simple pledge. A charity you choose. A ripple effect you create. Pledge 1% of your creator income to a cause that matters to you, and make your giving visible.

Become a Pioneer See how it works

Free to join. No donation is required to register.

Pledge. Join. Get recognized.

You decide how to calculate your 1% and when to give. We never ask for your income or payment details.

  1. Pledge

    Choose a charity you care about and pledge 1% of your creator income. It's voluntary.

  2. Join

    Add your details and social accounts, pick how public you want to be, and submit.

  3. Get recognized

    Right after you join you get your Pioneer ID and an optional pledge certificate to share.

After you join, you choose what's next.

Two things, in either order, and both are up to you.

  • Give directly Donate to your chosen charity through its own giving channel. II doesn't process or redistribute your 1%, and proof isn't required for Pioneer status.
  • Share and invite Post in your own voice, share your pledge or certificate if you wish, and encourage fellow creators to join you.

A pledge, not a payment.

Pioneer recognition is based on your pledge, not donation verification. You choose whether to appear publicly; anonymous Pioneers are counted but never identified. We may ask you to verify your email to protect the registry. Verification doesn't delay your pledge certificate.
