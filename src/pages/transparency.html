How giving works, in plain words.

Here’s exactly what we do, what we don’t, and how we protect your choices.

Become a Pioneer Read our privacy notice
The creator’s 1% pledge

Goes directly to the charity you choose.

The creator selects a charity and donates directly to it. During the Pioneer Stage, Influencers Involved does not collect, process, or redistribute the creator’s pledged 1%.

Donations to Influencers Involved

Support our nonprofit work.

Gifts through the Donate page go to {{legalName}} and support its nonprofit work, outreach, technology, and program development. You can give by card through Zeffy from the United States or abroad.

Donate to II

Honest, voluntary and in your hands.

  • Trust-based. The Pioneer Stage is voluntary. Creators may join before making their direct donation, and no proof is required.
  • A pledge, not a receipt. A Pioneer Certificate documents the pledge only. It is not a charitable contribution receipt or evidence that any payment occurred.
  • Your permission first. Public profiles require both creator permission and a verified email.
  • Anonymous means anonymous. Anonymous Pioneers count in aggregate but are not identified.
  • Private stays private. II does not publish private email, collaboration interests, or unapproved social profiles.
  • Not proof of donations. Registration records are not proof of donations.
