# GivingTuesday 2026: Four Animal-Related Charity Campaigns to Explore

*If animal welfare is close to your heart, these organizations have published specific plans for December 1, 2026. Here's what each one is asking supporters to help accomplish.*

There are many ways to take part in GivingTuesday, and many kinds of causes worth supporting. For this first roundup, we focused on **four animal-related organizations with public 2026 campaign information** that readers can verify for themselves.

They aren't ranked, and this isn't an endorsement of one nonprofit over another. The organizations do different work, from cat rescue and shelter care to training assistance dogs. Each is an independent organization; you should review its own materials before making a gift.

## At a glance: four public campaigns

| Organization | What its 2026 campaign emphasizes | Official campaign information |
|---|---|---|
| SAFE Haven for Cats — Raleigh, North Carolina | A December 1 triple-match opportunity described as up to $30,000, tied to cat care and adoption stories | [SAFE Haven's GivingTuesday page](https://www.safehavenforcats.org/news/giving-tuesday/) |
| Save The Animals Foundation — Cincinnati, Ohio | Its **#JustOneMore** appeal for dogs and cats needing shelter, care and second chances | [STAF's GivingTuesday page](https://staf.org/giving-tuesday/) |
| Dogs for Better Lives — Oregon and Massachusetts campuses, national reach | A $50,000 GivingTuesday goal to support the journey of a future Service Dog, with donations matched up to $25,000 | [2026 Giving Season Toolkit](https://dogsforbetterlives.org/social-media-toolkit-2026/) |
| Operation Pets Alive — Montgomery County, Texas | Donations supporting medical care, food, beds, toys and animal transport; the organization also asks people to share the campaign | [Operation Pets Alive's 2026 event](https://operationpetsalive.org/event/giving-tuesday-2026/) |

*Campaign details are taken from the organizations' published pages reviewed on November 19, 2026. Matches and goals are time-sensitive; confirm terms on the official site before donating.*

## SAFE Haven for Cats: a campaign built around stories

SAFE Haven for Cats in Raleigh works in cat rescue, adoption, spay/neuter services and community programs. Its GivingTuesday campaign is called **Every Cat Has a Story**. The group has introduced a playful set of fictional book-cover concepts inspired by six real cats in its care, using a creative theme to draw attention to real animal needs.

On December 1, SAFE Haven says qualifying gifts will be tripled, up to a stated $30,000 campaign limit. If you're interested in this opportunity, check the details on its own site. Read our [closer look at SAFE Haven's campaign](/editorial/safe-haven-for-cats-givingtuesday-2026/).

## Save The Animals Foundation: room for one more

Cincinnati-based **Save The Animals Foundation**, known as STAF, is a volunteer-operated, no-kill rescue and sanctuary for cats and dogs. Its **#JustOneMore** GivingTuesday theme asks what it takes to be ready for one more animal that needs help.

The organization has introduced Rex, a rescued dog, and Bubba, a senior cat, as its 2026 campaign spokesanimals. Their stories put faces to a less visible part of rescue work: daily care continues whether or not a fundraiser is trending. Here's our [STAF GivingTuesday spotlight](/editorial/staf-just-one-more-givingtuesday-2026/).

## Dogs for Better Lives: what goes into preparing a Service Dog

Dogs for Better Lives trains and places assistance dogs, including Hearing Assistance Dogs, Autism Assistance Dogs and Facility Dogs. Its published 2026 toolkit sets a **$50,000 GivingTuesday goal** for the journey of a future Service Dog and describes a donor match up to **$25,000**.

For readers interested in the connection between animals, accessibility and independence, this campaign offers a concrete look at why training and follow-up are part of the cost of service-dog placement. Read our [Dogs for Better Lives feature](/editorial/dogs-for-better-lives-givingtuesday-2026/).

## Operation Pets Alive: practical needs in Montgomery County

Operation Pets Alive describes itself as an all-volunteer nonprofit working toward a no-kill community in Montgomery County, Texas. Its 2026 GivingTuesday page names everyday needs: veterinary care, food, beds, toys and transport for animals in its care.

The organization also explicitly invites people who cannot donate to **share its campaign**. For someone with an audience interested in rescue work, that's a clear, low-pressure way to help others find the official request. [See Operation Pets Alive's 2026 GivingTuesday event](https://operationpetsalive.org/event/giving-tuesday-2026/).

## How to decide which campaign fits you

Consider the part of the work that matters most to you. Are you drawn to local shelter care? Rehabilitation and adoption? Assistance dogs working with people? Then visit the organization's official site, learn how its program works and check any matching-gift terms.

If you want more guidance, use our [seven-question charity checklist](/editorial/how-to-choose-a-givingtuesday-charity/). You can also support another cause entirely. This collection reflects the publicly documented campaigns we reviewed, not an attempt to catalog every GivingTuesday nonprofit.

## Sharing a campaign responsibly

Creators and community pages can help people discover these appeals without claiming a formal collaboration. Say where your information came from, link to the relevant charity, and explain why the work resonates with you. Don't borrow the charity's photos or logos automatically, and don't describe unconfirmed totals as results.
