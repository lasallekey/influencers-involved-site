# SAFE Haven for Cats Gives Its Rescue Cats a New Kind of Story This GivingTuesday

*The Raleigh nonprofit's 2026 campaign combines a playful book-club idea with a one-day triple-match opportunity on December 1.*

At an animal shelter, a cat's story doesn't begin when a visitor meets them. There is the life before the shelter, the day the cat arrives, the care it needs, the waiting, and sometimes the slow process of learning to trust again.

**SAFE Haven for Cats**, a Raleigh, North Carolina nonprofit, is leaning into the idea that every animal has a story for **GivingTuesday 2026, Tuesday, December 1**. Its campaign pairs the theme **Every Cat Has a Story** with a lighthearted creative project: six cats recast as the stars of imagined books.

It's a smart way to invite people to pay attention, but beneath the fictional covers is the real work of rescue, care and adoption. [SAFE Haven describes the project and its campaign on its website](https://www.safehavenforcats.org/news/giving-tuesday/).

## What does SAFE Haven for Cats do?

Founded in 1994, SAFE Haven operates a no-kill cat shelter and low-cost spay/neuter services in the Raleigh area. Its stated mission includes rescue, adoption, education and community programs. Those are connected pieces of animal welfare: helping cats in need now while also working to reduce future overpopulation and homelessness. You can read more about the organization's [mission and programs](https://www.safehavenforcats.org/about-us/).

For someone who has adopted a cat, the shelter visit may be the memorable part. But food, medical attention, safe accommodation, socialization and the people who keep that routine going are what make an adoption possible.

## A Purrfect Book Club, with real cats behind the covers

SAFE Haven calls its GivingTuesday storytelling project **The Purrfect Book Club: Six Tales of Love, Hope & Nine Lives**. Rather than publish ordinary appeal posters alone, the group has imagined its cats as characters in genres ranging from fantasy and romance to mystery.

It's an amusing idea with a practical purpose. The book-cover premise makes someone pause long enough to notice an animal who might otherwise be one more photo in an endless feed. Then the charity brings the reader back to the real animal and the care behind the story.

We won't reproduce the campaign artwork here. You can [see the original creative work directly on SAFE Haven's website](https://www.safehavenforcats.org/news/giving-tuesday/), where the organization presents the characters and the associated campaign.

## How does the December 1 matching opportunity work?

SAFE Haven says that on **December 1, 2026**, GivingTuesday gifts will be **tripled, up to $30,000**, for that day only. Its official event page illustrates this with examples: a $25 gift becoming $75, a $50 gift becoming $150, and a $100 gift becoming $300 under the campaign terms. Those are the organization's published examples, not an independently verified calculation of gifts already received.

Matching opportunities can have limits and conditions. Anyone considering donating should read the current information on [SAFE Haven's GivingTuesday event page](https://www.safehavenforcats.org/event-sh/giving-tuesday-2026/) and use the charity's own donation route. We have not independently verified a sponsor's funding or how much of the match, if any, has already been used.

## How can supporters help?

**Read the cats' stories.** The point of this campaign is not only the match. Following the cats' experiences can tell you something about the patience and resources that shelter care requires.

**Give through the official organization, if you're able.** The campaign page describes how donations support care such as food, shelter and medical treatment. Check the live terms if a match influences your decision.

**Share accurate campaign information.** Know someone who loves cats or regularly supports rescue groups? Send them the official page with a short personal note, and mention the December 1 date accurately. A good story is worth passing along on its own.

**Consider involvement beyond one day.** Adoption, volunteering and other support options are described on SAFE Haven's main site. The information is there for anyone who wants a longer relationship with the cause.

## Why this is an interesting campaign for creators

The book-club concept is a useful reminder that nonprofit storytelling doesn't always need to be solemn to be sincere. A compelling frame can help someone stop scrolling. The facts and the animals still need to carry the meaning.

For a book reviewer or cat-loving creator, this campaign may offer a natural conversation starter: what makes an animal's story memorable, and what does it take for a shelter to help write the next chapter?
